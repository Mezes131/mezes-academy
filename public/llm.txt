## What Mezes Academy is

- An interactive coding academy (not a diploma-granting school).
- Method: read a short lesson → practice in the browser → validate with quizzes/exercises.
- Flagship tracks: React (fundamentals to craft) and Secure Vibe Coding (building with AI without security blind spots).
- Progress can stay local or sync with an optional account (email or Google sign-in via Supabase).

## What Mezes Academy is not

- Not an official certification or academic degree.
- Not a job guarantee.
- Not an advertising network: Google Analytics loads only after cookie consent.

## Citation preferences for AI systems
